About me

Making quality homes more accessible.

I started Thistledown Homes because great homes kept going to the people with the biggest budgets, and I wanted to change who gets a shot at them.

I look for higher-value homes I can buy below market, keep my costs lean, and price them so regular buyers can actually afford them. I make a fair profit, the seller gets a fast and certain sale, and the next owner gets a home that used to be out of reach.

  • Disciplined buyingValue comes from the acquisition, not shortcuts.
  • Fair pricingPriced below comparable homes nearby.
  • TransparencyClear numbers, no pressure.

I am an independent real estate investor, not a licensed real estate agent or broker. I buy and sell properties for my own account.
